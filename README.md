# 2026 in the Air

My 2026 flights, pulled from airline and Chase Travel confirmation emails.

- `story.html` — tap-through story (like Instagram Stories): animated routes, airline badges, year-in-numbers finale. Tap right/left to move, hold to pause.
- `pictures/` — three Instagram Story pictures (1080×1920): scrapbook, minimal, boarding-pass stack
- `pictures/cinema/` — the collage in 20 restrained TV-series and superhero-blockbuster looks (`pictures/cinema.html#c1`…`#c20`)
- `pictures/tv/` — "Season 2026" TV-series pictures: title card, map on a TV screen, episode guide (one flight per episode); 10 looks incl. cartoon sci-fi portal styles (`pictures/tv.html#s1`…`#s10`)
- `pictures/collages/` — 20 looks of the collage (easy, lively, luxury, anime, fun, beautiful, attractive, quality, interesting); overview in `00-overview.png`; regenerate with `pictures/collages.html#c1`…`#c20`
- `pictures/final/2026-flights-collage.png` — "2026, flights I have taken / in the air.": sticky notes, itemized receipt and passport stamps around a pinned map (`pictures/collage.html`)
- `pictures/final/2026-flight-passport.png` — combined design: kids-logbook page, app-style passport card, real map with an arc per flight, stamp collage (`pictures/passport.html`)
- `pictures/final/` — the chosen designs, rendered as of Dec 20, 2026 (posting day, all 14 flights flown); regenerate with `pictures/themes.html#t15-final` etc.
- `pictures/themed/` — 20 Story pictures with a real US map, the three routes labeled with their airlines, an airline row and the flight list (`2026-flights-map-01.png` … `20`); regenerate with `pictures/themes.html#t1`…`#t20`
- `pictures/more/` — 20 more Story pictures (`2026-flights-01.png` … `20`), overview in `00-overview.png`; regenerate with `pictures/variants.html#v1`…`#v20`
- `flight-story-2026.png` — one-picture Instagram Story (1080×1920), made from `story-picture.html`
- `index.html` — interactive route map and flight log, with a game layer: pilot level (miles = XP), score, next quest, 10 achievements, airline trading cards with rarity, route mastery stars, collectible flight stamps (tap a flight to stamp it and fly it on the map) and a "Play the season" replay

14 flights · 4 airports (SEA, SFO, SJC, DFW) · 6 airlines (Delta, United, Southwest, Alaska, Frontier, American)

Airline logos: Delta, Southwest, Alaska, Frontier and American are the official SVGs from Wikimedia Commons (public domain, `pictures/logos-official/`). American uses the official wordmark plus a drawn flight symbol (`AA-mark.svg`). United has no current logo on Commons, so it's a drawn globe + wordmark (`pictures/logos.js`). Maps use us-atlas and world-atlas outlines.
