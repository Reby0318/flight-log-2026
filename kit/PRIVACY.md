# Before You Post or Share

A flight log says a lot about you. Check these before posting a picture or making a repo or page public.

- **Upcoming trips:** posting future dates tells everyone when you'll be away from home. Post after you're back, or remove upcoming flights.
- **Confirmation codes:** never include them. With a code and your last name, anyone can open and change your booking.
- **Barcodes:** use decorative barcodes only, never a real boarding-pass barcode (it can contain your booking details).
- **Card digits, prices, loyalty numbers:** leave them out.
- **Your full name and home airport:** together with dates, they make you easy to find. Consider first name only.
- **Other people:** don't include companions' names or their bookings without asking.
- **Public links:** a shared page or public repo can be copied and indexed. Set it to private until you're sure.
